## 📖 About the App

**BondLens** is an advanced, AI-driven legal and business document risk analyzer built to simplify how professionals, founders, and individuals review critical agreements. Navigating dense corporate paperwork, service contracts, and legal terms can be tedious, high-risk, and expensive. 

BondLens solves this by acting as an intelligent co-pilot that:
* **Scans & Evaluates:** Rapidly reads through legal and business documents to spot hidden loopholes, unfair liability clauses, and potential red flags.
* **Simplifies Complex Jargon:** Converts dense, confusing legal terminology into plain, easy-to-understand business language.
* **Provides Risk Insights:** Helps stakeholders make well-informed decisions by highlighting areas that require negotiation or closer attention before signing.
